Zombie Rush - audio credits
===========================

All sounds below are public domain (CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
No credit is required; listed here for the record. Files were trimmed, faded, normalised
and converted to 44.1 kHz for the game.

Guns (SFX/Weapons/*_shot_*) - "The Free Firearm Sound Library" (Prepared SFX Library),
  https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
  pistol = Colt 1911, rifle = AR-15, shotgun = Winchester Model 12 / Benelli Nova.

Zombies (SFX/Zombies/zombie_*) - "Zombies Sound Pack" by artisticdude,
  https://opengameart.org/content/zombies-sound-pack
  and "Zombie noises and moans" by ianzazz,
  https://opengameart.org/content/zombie-noises-and-moans

Impacts, UI, turret, jingles, coin, reload (all other SFX) - Kenney (www.kenney.nl):
  Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, Sci-Fi Sounds, Music Jingles, Casino Audio.

Music:
  Music/menu_music.ogg  - "Chill Main Menu music", https://opengameart.org/node/3976
  Music/game_music.ogg  - "Dark Cavern Ambient" (track 002), https://opengameart.org/node/131394
