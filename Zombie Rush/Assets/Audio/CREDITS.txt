Zombie Rush - audio credits
===========================

All sounds below are public domain (CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
No credit is required; listed here for the record. Files were trimmed, faded, normalised
and converted to 44.1 kHz for the game.

Guns (SFX/Weapons/*_shot_*) - "The Free Firearm Sound Library" (Prepared SFX Library),
  https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
  pistol = Colt 1911, rifle = AR-15, shotgun = Winchester Model 12 / Benelli Nova.

Zombies (SFX/Zombies/zombie_*) - "Zombies Sound Pack" by artisticdude,
  https://opengameart.org/content/zombies-sound-pack
  and "Zombie noises and moans" by ianzazz,
  https://opengameart.org/content/zombie-noises-and-moans

Impacts, UI, turret, jingles, coin, reload (all other SFX) - Kenney (www.kenney.nl):
  Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, Sci-Fi Sounds, Music Jingles, Casino Audio.

Music:
  Music/menu_music.ogg  - "Chill Main Menu music", https://opengameart.org/node/3976
  Music/game_music.ogg  - "Dark Cavern Ambient" (track 002), https://opengameart.org/node/131394
  Music/combat_music.ogg - "Post-Apocalyptic Night" by MintoDog (plays during waves),
    https://opengameart.org/content/post-apocalyptic-morning-afternoon-and-night

Added 2026-11-13 (all CC0; reloads were cut and re-timed to the reload animations):
  SFX/Weapons/reload_pistol.wav  - "Handgun Reload Sound Effect" by zer0_sol,
    https://opengameart.org/content/handgun-reload-sound-effect
  SFX/Weapons/reload_rifle.wav   - "Gun reload sounds" by SpringySpringo (assault rifle reload),
    https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds
  SFX/Weapons/reload_shotgun.wav - "Shotgun Reload Sound effects" by zer0_sol (shells), the snap shut
    from "Gun reload sounds" by SpringySpringo, the latch and falling shells from Kenney (RPG Audio, Impact Sounds),
    https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-reload-sound-effects
  SFX/Weapons/weapon_draw_*.wav  - Kenney RPG Audio (cloth, metal click, belt, latch), www.kenney.nl
  SFX/Player/footstep_*.wav      - Kenney Impact Sounds (concrete and grass footsteps), www.kenney.nl
  SFX/Player/heartbeat.wav       - "Heartbeat sounds" by bart, https://opengameart.org/content/heartbeat-sounds
  Ambience/night_ambience.ogg    - "wind1" by Luke.RUSTLTD, https://opengameart.org/content/wind1
    and "Crickets Ambient Noise - loopable" by Wolfgang_,
    https://opengameart.org/content/crickets-ambient-noise-loopable
  SFX/Zombies/goo_spit.wav, goo_splat.wav - moved out of _Archive (same sources as before).
